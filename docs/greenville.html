A Letter to Our Greenville Patients

Dear Juniper Family,

After much thought and consideration, we've made the difficult decision to close our Greenville office. Our last day seeing patients will be January 15, 2027.

For the past several years, it has been such a privilege to care for your families. We've celebrated pregnancies, welcomed new babies, watched little ones grow, and walked alongside so many of you through different seasons of life. The relationships we've built are what have made Juniper so special, and we are incredibly grateful for the trust you've placed in us.

Continuing Your Chiropractic Care

We want to make this transition as smooth as possible for you and your family. We've personally spoken with the following chiropractors, who have training and approaches to care similar to ours. Each office has confirmed that they are accepting new patients and has graciously agreed to extend a discount to Juniper patients to help ease the transition.

  • Active Family ChiropracticDr. Haven Wood | (864) 223-2236

You're welcome to contact these offices directly to learn more about their care, available discounts, and scheduling.

Existing Care Plans and Packages

If you currently have an active care plan or prepaid package, we encourage you to use your remaining visits before January 15, 2027.

Any unused prepaid packages will be reviewed individually. Refunds will be calculated by applying our regular individual visit rate to the visits already used, with any remaining balance refunded to you.

If you have questions about your care plan or package, please contact our office. We're happy to review your account with you.

Your Patient Records

Your patient records will remain securely maintained by Juniper Family Chiropractic at our North Augusta location, under the care of Dr. Ashley Felak.

If you would like a copy of your records or would like them sent to another healthcare provider, simply complete the attached records release form and return it to us at juniperfamilychiro@gmail.com.

Please allow up to 14 calendar days for us to process your request. Your records will remain available after our Greenville office closes, so there is no need to request them before January 15, 2027.

A Personal Thank You from Dr. Ashley

As I reflect on my years in Greenville, I realize just how much you have given me. I may have been your chiropractor, but you have taught me so much about motherhood, family, perseverance, and grace. I am a better, more well-rounded mother because of the stories you've shared, the experiences you've walked through, and the lessons I've had the privilege of learning from you.

I hope you know that I take you home with me. I think of you often. Your stories come to mind as I navigate my own motherhood, and I find myself drawing on lessons I've learned through your experiences. You've shaped not only the doctor I am, but the mother and woman I've become.

Thank you for trusting me with your families. Thank you for sharing your stories, your joys, your struggles, and your little ones with me. Thank you for making my work so joyful. Thank you for making it easy to show up each day. And thank you for making it so very hard to leave.

In the words of Winnie the Pooh: "How lucky I am to have something that makes saying goodbye so hard."

I couldn't have said it better myself.

With all my love and gratitude,
Dr. Ashley
