Webster care

A gentle approach to pelvic biomechanics.

The Webster Technique is a specific chiropractic analysis and adjustment approach used during pregnancy. Care is directed toward sacral and pelvic joint function, with the goal of improving comfort and reducing musculoskeletal imbalance.

Webster care is not a technique for turning a baby and does not replace obstetric or midwifery care.

Birth preparation

Power, Passage & Passenger

These three ideas are often used to describe the physical components of birth. Chiropractic care can be one piece of a broader prenatal support team.

Power

Your body's muscular and nervous system function changes throughout pregnancy. Chiropractic care focuses on musculoskeletal comfort and movement so you can move through pregnancy with fewer physical restrictions.

Passage

Pelvic joints, muscles, and ligaments all contribute to the space and mechanics of the pelvis. Care is aimed at supporting balanced pelvic movement and comfort.

Passenger

Baby's position is influenced by many factors. Webster care focuses on the mother's pelvic biomechanics rather than directly attempting to reposition baby.

Pregnancy FAQ

What parents often ask us.

Is chiropractic care safe during pregnancy?

Chiropractic care can be modified for pregnancy with gentle techniques and positioning supports. Tell us about your pregnancy, symptoms, and any medical concerns so care can be individualized appropriately.

Can chiropractic care help with pregnancy discomfort?

Many pregnant patients seek chiropractic care for back, hip, pelvic, and postural discomfort. Your exam helps determine whether chiropractic care is appropriate for your specific concerns.

Does Webster Technique turn breech babies?

No. Webster Technique is directed at pelvic and sacral function in the mother. It is not an obstetric procedure and does not directly turn or reposition a baby.
