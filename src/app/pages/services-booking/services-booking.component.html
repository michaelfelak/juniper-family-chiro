Your Visits

Initial Examination

For new patients or patients returning after more than a year away. Your initial visit gives us time to learn about your health history, discuss your concerns and goals, complete an examination, and determine the best recommendations for your care.

Reassessment

For current patients who need their progress or care recommendations reassessed. Required if you have not been seen in over 6 months or have recently completed your initial care plan.

Adjustment

For returning patients continuing their recommended care.

Care Options

Acute Care

For patients who need more frequent care for a period of time. Your recommended frequency is based on your examination findings, individual needs, and goals for care.

Wellness Care

For individuals who choose to continue regular chiropractic care after their initial care plan, with a focus on supporting nervous system function and maintaining the progress they’ve made.

Family Wellness

Ongoing care designed for families who want to stay under chiropractic care together.

Family Visit

For family members receiving care together during the same visit.

12-Visit Package

A flexible option for returning patients and families. Visits do not expire and may be shared among family members.

Reduced-Cost Options

We believe every family deserves access to quality chiropractic care. Reduced-cost care may be available for families who qualify.
