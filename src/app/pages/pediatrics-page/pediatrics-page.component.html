Gentle care for growing bodies.

Why parents bring children in

01

Birth & Infancy

Birth is one of the first major physical stresses a baby experiences. Whether a baby is born vaginally or by cesarean, the birth process can place stress on a newborn’s neck and developing nervous system. Gentle chiropractic care helps address tension and restore balance as your baby adapts to life outside the womb.

What can tension look like in a baby?

Tension patterns in babies can show up in ways you may not immediately connect to their neck or spine.

  • Preferring to look to one side
  • Nursing better on one side
  • Arching the back
  • Difficulty burping
  • Seeming tense or uncomfortable
02

Growth & Milestones

Rolling, sitting, crawling, walking, and running are exciting milestones that require your child’s body and nervous system to continually adapt to new skills. Gentle chiropractic care supports healthy movement and nervous system function as your child develops coordination, balance, and confidence in their growing body.

What can this look like?

  • Atypical gait or posture
  • Favoring one side
  • Delayed milestones
  • Sleep disturbances
  • Grinding or clenching teeth
  • Difficulty with teething
  • Emotional outbursts
03

Everyday Childhood

Kids are made to move, play, explore, and occasionally take a tumble. Falls, playground adventures, bike rides, sports, heavy backpacks, and hours spent sitting at school all place different demands on a growing body. Chiropractic care supports healthy movement and helps the nervous system adapt to the physical stresses that come with being a kid.

What can this look like?

  • Growing pains or body aches
  • Headaches
  • Difficulty sleeping or settling at night
  • Changes in posture
  • Discomfort after sports, falls, or rough play
  • Difficulty regulating emotions
  • Trouble focusing or sitting still

The Whole Child

Children experience more than just physical stress. Their nervous systems are constantly adapting to physical, chemical, and emotional stressors as they grow. Chiropractic care supports the nervous system and your child’s ability to adapt through every season of childhood.

Physical

Growth spurts, falls, sports, repetitive activities, prolonged sitting, and everyday play continually place new demands on a growing body. Supporting healthy movement and nervous system function helps children adapt to those physical demands.

Chemical

Food, medications, illness, cleaning products, and other environmental exposures are all things a child’s body must process and respond to. These chemical stressors are another part of what a developing nervous system continually adapts to.

Emotional

Starting school, changes in routine, new siblings, friendships, growing independence, and eventually puberty all bring new emotions and experiences for a child’s nervous system to process and adapt to.

What to Expect

Adjusting a baby or child looks very different from adjusting an adult. Our pediatric care is specific and designed with growing bodies in mind.

1

Gentle Techniques

Pediatric adjustments use gentle, specific pressure—about six ounces, similar to the amount of pressure you would use to check a tomato for ripeness. Techniques are always adapted to your child’s age, size, and comfort.

2

Child-Led Visits

We believe in building trust and respecting your child’s body autonomy. If your child isn’t feeling it that day, that’s okay! We’ll never force an adjustment or adjust a child against their will. We can play, get comfortable with the space, and try again another day. Our goal is for your child to feel safe, respected, and comfortable in our office.

3

Clear Communication

We want you to understand what we’re seeing and why we’re making our recommendations. We’ll explain what we find, answer your questions, and make sure you feel comfortable with your child’s care.

Collaborative Care

We believe children are best supported when their care team works together. When appropriate, we’re happy to collaborate with your child’s pediatrician, lactation consultant, therapist, or other healthcare providers to help support your family’s goals.

Pediatric FAQ

What parents often ask us.

Is chiropractic care safe for babies and children?

Yes. Pediatric chiropractic care uses techniques specifically adapted to your child’s age, size, and stage of development.

How soon can my baby be adjusted?

Babies can receive chiropractic care from birth. Because birth itself can place stress on a newborn’s neck and nervous system, some families choose to have their baby checked within the first days or weeks of life.

Will you pop or crack my child’s neck or back?

No. Pediatric adjustments do not involve the popping or cracking you may associate with adult chiropractic adjustments. We use specific, low-force techniques appropriate for your child’s age and size. For babies, we use about six ounces of pressure—similar to the amount you would use to check a tomato for ripeness.

Why might I bring my baby to a chiropractor?

Parents bring their babies to us for many different reasons. Sometimes they notice tension patterns, such as preferring to look or nurse to one side. Other families come in with concerns about fussiness at the breast or bottle, spitting up, constipation, or sleep issues. All can be signs of stress. We’ll listen to what you’re noticing, evaluate your baby, and explain what we find.

Does my child have to have a problem to see a chiropractor?

No, many families choose chiropractic care as part of their child’s overall wellness. The nervous system plays an important role in coordinating the functions throughout the body, including communicating with the immune and endocrine systems. Our goal with chiropractic care is to support healthy nervous system function as your child grows, develops, and adapts to the physical, chemical, and emotional stressors of everyday life.

What if my child doesn’t want to be adjusted?

That’s okay! We believe in respecting your child’s body autonomy and building trust. We will never force an adjustment or adjust a child against their will. Sometimes the best visit is simply about getting comfortable with the office, building trust, and trying again another day.
