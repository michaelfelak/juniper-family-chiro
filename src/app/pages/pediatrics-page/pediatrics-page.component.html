The whole child

Three kinds of stress that can affect how kids feel.

Emotional

Big feelings, poor sleep, new routines, and stress can influence muscle tension, posture, and the way children carry themselves. We consider these factors as part of the overall picture.

Physical

Falls, sports, backpacks, prolonged sitting, growth spurts, and repetitive movement can affect comfort and mobility. A chiropractic exam looks at how the spine and joints are moving.

Environmental

Sleep, hydration, nutrition, routines, and the child's broader environment can influence how they feel day to day. These factors are important context, even though chiropractic care does not treat allergies, toxicity, or medical disease.

What to expect

Pediatric adjustments are different from adult adjustments.

1

We start with history and observation.

We talk about your concerns, development, activity, and any relevant medical history.

2

We assess movement and function.

The exam is adapted for age and may include posture, range of motion, and gentle palpation.

3

Care is gentle and individualized.

When chiropractic care is appropriate, techniques are selected for the child's age, size, and comfort.

Our role

Chiropractic is one part of a child's care team.

We do not replace your pediatrician, therapist, lactation consultant, or other healthcare providers. When a concern is outside our scope, we refer or coordinate with the appropriate professional.
